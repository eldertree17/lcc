Cricket is what brings us together, but the social calendar is what keeps us together — from beach sixes in Mui Wo to black-tie balls, brewery tours and overseas adventures.

Feb 2023

LCC vs HKU — Match Report

On a sunny day at Sandy Bay the two captains amicably decided to forego the toss and agreed that Lamma CC would bowl first — for three important reasons: make sure we get through 20 overs, chasing should be easier on a small ground, and we wanted to get the hard part of the game out of the way and get onto the beers sooner.

The classic Sandy Bay rules were in effect: no sixes (hit one and you’re out), max 3 overs per bowler, 25 run retirement and max 10 balls in an over. HKU posted 146 before we chased, reaching 125/8 — falling short by 21 runs but having an absolute blast in the process.

Positives: Good batting by Vikas and Rajesh, solid bowling and fielding, and playing the game in golden spirit. Areas for improvement: 31 wides and 4 wickets in an over. Always tough to win after that!

Nov 2019

Yardley Brothers Brewery Tour

Friday, 1 November 2019 — A Friday evening brewery tour of our good sponsors Yardley Brothers was too good to pass up. Not the easiest place to find, hidden in the industrial area of Kwai Chung, but once in their cosy brewery — and cosier bar — the travelling was well worth it.

Yardley Bros turn out some fantastic craft beer and some off-the-wall crazy ones too (the Thai Chilli Getaway Sour and the Cardamom Wheat come to mind). Our Chairman made a presentation speech and we presented an LCC pennant, which was very well received. Chucker proved he could organise a p**s-up in a brewery.

A big thanks to Luke and his team for taking such good care of us until late in the evening. We shall certainly look forward to gathering in the Yardley Bros Beer Shack in Yung Shue Wan. Cheers!

Oct 2019

Big Bash Beach Cricket Sixes

First game: 11:00am, Saturday 12 October. Five teams in a round-robin, with the top two playing in The Castle Cup Final and third vs fourth for the plastic spade.

Teams: Mui Wo Mutineers (Cap’n Brent) · AB Buccaneers (Cap’n Benny) · Yardley Swashbucklers (Cap’n Just) · DB Sea Lions (Cap’n Nilesh) · DB Manta Rays (Cap’n Kev)

For an unprecedented 4th consecutive time the Mui Wo Mutineers are Lamma CC’s Big Bash Beach Cricket champions. A superb double over from last man standing Seany Boy saw the Mutineers through in similar circumstances to last year. Another great LCC day out — the golden castle dedicated to the memory of Richie Abbott.

Club Tradition

The Dish of Desire

“The dish made me do it.”

Handed down through generations, this dish of beaten Nepalese tin is purportedly taken from the same mines of the deepest valley between the highest mountains that birthed Dennis Lillee’s aluminium cricket bat. It has taken 700 years to arrive here at the Lamma Cricket Club.

It has seen war, gold rushes and Taipans and has been used to measure the weight of dried tiger sperm and opium. It has seen service as a dog dish and been owned by a Bangkok banker with a wooden cock. When asked “why?” sweat would break out upon the owner’s face and he would say with a feverish twitch, rubbing his forehead in anguish, “The dish made me do it”.

Some say it is cursed and others say it has an ancient magic, and when it swings in the wind those same people say some can hear the soul of the sad banker’s wicked old wife cackling at her husband’s plight, her shadowy shape forever embedded in its mysterious dull shine.

To stare at the Mug’s Mug too long is to begin speaking in tongues and if perchance it be handed to some unlucky grass faerie posing as a cricket player, he or she will feel an unpleasant tingling in their crotch. A deep festering and putrid smelling wound will appear within 4 days upon the unlucky person’s place of fun and will resist all antibiotics known in medical circles unless that person partake upon a quest.

The quest is: to find the secret meaning of cricket — but not just any type of cricket, but cricket played on Lamma Island. The answer can only be found somewhere between the bottom of a pint of beer at its birth place and the famed concrete stumps built by the harnessers of power. The quest may last for 13 days (one day for each team-mate and including 12th man and scorer) and all will know if the unlucky grass faerie has found the secret, because he (or she) will shout it from the hilltops while wearing nothing but a stubby holder over the genitalia and a red mark shall be displayed upon their forehead symbolizing where the old Bangkok Banker glued his shriveled dismembered member to the forehead and pranced underneath the moon declaring they are a unicorn.

Then, and only then, will the unpleasant tingling stop and the wound upon his or her genital be alleviated. But they will still be a mug until the next mug is given the challenge and then the red mark will be transferred to that mug’s head.

Beware the Mug’s Mug and the shadowy laughter of the old crone’s soul for it is bat-shit evil. You have been warned. Hideous things may happen — be afraid, very afraid.
