Welcome to Lamma Cricket Club!

Founded on the principles of Solidarity, Sustainability, and Pride, our Club is more than just a place to play—it is a community built on respect, fair competition, and genuine camaraderie. We invite players and supporters to join us in promoting the integrity of cricket, competing with heart but never at the cost of sportsmanship.

LCC

Established 1990 · Lamma Island, Hong Kong

Mateship through cricket

At Lamma CC we pride ourselves on our ethos of mateship through cricket. In many ways the friendships we form off the field are just as important — if not more important — than the cricket we play on it.
