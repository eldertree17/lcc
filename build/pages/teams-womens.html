Rooted in community

Founded in 1992, Lamma Cricket Club has grown into one of Hong Kong’s most distinctive cricketing institutions — men’s, women’s and social teams, all defined by a community-first spirit and those iconic green and yellow colours. At the heart of the women’s game are the Lamma Roses.
