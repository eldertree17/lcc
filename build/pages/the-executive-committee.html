Roles & Responsibilities

What each member of the Executive Committee is responsible for.

Executive CommitteeChairman
  • Represents the Club and seeks its best interests.
  • Coordinates and leads the Club’s Executive Committee in pursuit of the Club’s agreed objectives.
Executive CommitteeClub Manager
  • Seeks, provides and oversees opportunities for LCC members to enjoy social cricket matches (usually, but not limited to HKCC, KCC and HKU fixtures).
  • Appoints a Captain for social fixtures (can be himself or another Member).
  • Coordinates organising committees for overseas tours.
  • Coordinates and books nets (Vice Captains will manage attendance).
  • Coordinates Club coaching arrangements.
  • Responsible for leading the Friday Master’s League Team, ensuring all players adhere to the high standards of competitiveness LCC promote, combined with fair play and respect for our opponents, match officials and the game.
  • Nominates captain and vice-captain (nomination to be approved by the ExCom).
  • Decision maker on all aspects of the Friday Master’s League Team, including match day selection and preparation (in consultation with Vice Captain).
  • Ensures that all players selected are eligible for selection, i.e. paid up LCC members / CHK registered.
  • Ensures all match fees are collected.
Executive CommitteeSecretary
  • Responsible for official communications and representing the Club with outside organisations, including CHK.
  • Manages recruiting and membership offers.
  • Keeps a record of Membership numbers and league eligibility and registration.
  • Manages LCC ACUS communications.
  • Oversees all formal internal communications regarding policy, Executive Committee decisions, ECM and AGM meeting dates etc.
  • Minute taking and circulation of ECM and AGM minutes.
  • LCMC representative.
  • Coordinates end of season awards.
Executive CommitteeTreasurer
  • Responsible for management and accurate up to date reporting of Club finances.
  • Responsible for creating LCC budget plans and agendas.
  • Responsible for collection of fees (annual membership) and match fees from Captains.
Executive CommitteeEvents & Tours
  • Oversees all social events (usually, but not limited to Beach Cricket, Cricketer’s Ball, Intra-Lamma Match).
  • Communicates details regarding all social events and tours to Members.
  • Responsible for designing, ordering, storing and distributing all Club merchandise.
  • Maintains a stocktake of all Club merchandise.
  • Ensures that sponsor logos are appropriately displayed on Club clothing.
  • Responsible for the maintenance of the Club website.
Executive CommitteeSponsorship Manager
  • Responsible for attracting sponsors to the Club.
  • Liaises with Treasurer to ensure that sponsorship funds are deposited into the Club bank account.
  • Liaises with the Marketing, Merchandise & Events Manager to ensure that sponsor logos are appropriately displayed on Club clothing.
Executive CommitteeSaturday League Captain
  • Responsible for leading the Saturday League Team, ensuring all players adhere to the high standards of competitiveness LCC promote, combined with fair play and respect for our opponents, match officials and the game.
  • Nominates vice-captain (Captain’s nomination to be approved by the ExCom).
  • Decision maker on all aspects of the Saturday League Team, including match day selection and preparation (in consultation with Vice Captain).
  • Ensures that all players selected are eligible for selection, i.e. paid up LCC members / CHK registered.
  • Ensures all match fees are collected.
Executive CommitteeChallenge League Captains — Power & Vikings
  • Responsible for leading the Challenge League sides (Power & Vikings), ensuring all players adhere to the high standards of competitiveness LCC promote, combined with fair play and respect for our opponents, match officials and the game.
  • Nominates vice-captain (Captain’s nomination to be approved by the ExCom).
  • Decision maker on all aspects of the Challenge League Team, including match day selection and preparation (in consultation with Vice Captain).
  • Ensures that all players selected are eligible for selection, i.e. paid up LCC members / CHK registered.
  • Ensures all match fees are collected.
