Match Reports

30 Dec 2022

Lamma CC vs Kowloon Rugby

Lamma lost the toss and were asked to bowl. Vishal, playing his last game for LCC, opened the bowling and removed the opener via a fine running catch from debutant Tom Booth. Ben Cooper bowled excellent lines (0–4 off two tight overs) before Ricky, Royce and Nitesh kept the pressure on, with Samay and Tom Booth changing the game through the middle — Tom removing the dangerous Mehran and Subash. A Ricky/Brenton run-out helped restrict KRL to 181–8 off 35 overs.

Chasing, Promodh and Nitesh opened well — 89–2 off 15.1 before Vishal came in with intent, hitting the most fours of the innings. Tom Booth retired on 31 not out, Brenton and Samay finished the job in the 26th over. Lamma won by 2 wickets.

Notables

  • Tom Booth (debut): 4–19, great catch, 31 n.o.
  • Only 18 extras — all 9 bowlers contributed
  • Marcus: excellent leg spin
  • Promodh & Nitesh opening partnership
  • Vishal: brilliant final knock, retired on first Friday
  • Brenton: quick-fire 17 n.o.
Man of the Match: Tom Booth & Vishal Gupta (tied) — Vishal, thank you for being part of this great club. A pleasure to play with you.
3 Feb 2023

Lamma Islanders vs HKCC Qi Lin

On a warm afternoon at GDB, HKCC won the toss and elected to field. Lamma’s top order struggled early — 29–4 after Nitesh (7), Mary (2), Promodh (6) and Ricky (0) all fell cheaply. Dheeraj and Riggers steadied to drinks at 77–4, with Dheeraj retiring on 30. After a Riggers stumping at 82–5, Royce and Vineet put on a 61-run partnership (10 boundaries) to push Lamma to 187 all out off 34.1 overs.

HKCC chased smoothly — 79–0 after 14 overs, 92–0 at drinks. Ria Martins got the breakthrough on debut (1–29 off 5 — excellent). Despite the best efforts of Subash (0–19 off 4.1), HKCC won in the 31st over chasing 188.

Batting

  • Dheeraj: 41
  • Vineet: 32 n.o.

Bowling

  • Ria Martins: 1–29 (debut)
  • Riggers: 0–21 off 5
  • Subash: 0–19 off 4.1
We never gave up and kept fighting to the end. Middle order did well; our debutant made an excellent impression. Only 16 extras. Areas to work on: top order consistency, bowling lines and fielding.
