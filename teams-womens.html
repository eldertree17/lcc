Women’s Teams

Lamma CC has a proud history with women’s cricket, including multiple HKCA Women’s League and Cup titles. This section will be updated with current season details soon.

Our Women’s Cricket History

LCC women’s sides have competed at the highest level in Hong Kong. From our Club Records:

2007–08

  • HKCA Women’s League Champions
  • HKCA Woman Cricketer of the Year (Neisha Pratt)
  • HKCA Best Woman Batsman (Neisha Pratt)
  • HKCA Best Woman Bowler (Neisha Pratt)
  • HKCA Best Woman Wicket-keeper (Sam McIlwraith)

2006–07

  • HKCA Women’s League Champions
  • HKCA Women’s Cup Champions
  • HKCA Woman Cricketer of the Year (Neisha Pratt)
  • HKCA Best Woman Batsman (Neisha Pratt)
  • HKCA Best Woman Wicket-keeper (Emma Phillips)
  • HK Cricket Festival Women’s Cup Winners

2005–06

  • HKCA Women’s League Champions
  • HKCA Women’s Cup Champions
  • HKCA Woman Cricketer of the Year (Neisha Pratt & Natasha Miles)
  • HKCA Best Woman Batsman (Neisha Pratt)
  • HKCA Best Woman Wicket-keeper (Anna Crutchley)
