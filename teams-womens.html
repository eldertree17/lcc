Women’s Cricket · Lamma Cricket Club

The Lamma Roses

A legacy of passion, pride and partnership. Named for the beauty and resilience of the island they call home, the Roses are the heart of LCC’s women’s programme.

Blooming with pride, backed by the best club in Hong Kong.

The Lamma Roses proudly represent LCC in Cricket Hong Kong, China competitions — a journey of excellence, determination and unwavering club support.

Inclusive membershipDedicated women’s league memberships, including the official LCC player’s shirt.

Professional coachingExpert coaching and technical guidance provided throughout the season.

Club-wide spiritFull members of the LCC family — social events, tours and friendship through cricket.

Rooted in community

Founded in 1992, Lamma Cricket Club has grown into one of Hong Kong’s most distinctive cricketing institutions. With men’s, women’s and social teams, LCC is a cornerstone of the local cricket scene — its iconic green and yellow colours a symbol of the club’s community-first spirit. At the heart of the women’s programme are the Lamma Roses.

More than a team

The Roses embody what makes Lamma Cricket Club special: inclusivity, excellence and community. Whether competing on the field, touring overseas or inspiring the next generation, they carry the spirit of Lamma Island with them wherever they go.
