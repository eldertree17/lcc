Welcome to Lamma CC

Competitive and social cricket on Lamma Island since 1990 — a Division 1 league side, friendly fixtures and overseas tours, plus one of the best social calendars in the game, from beach cricket tournaments to black-tie balls.

LCC

Established 1990 · Lamma Island, Hong Kong

Mateship through cricket

At Lamma CC we pride ourselves on our ethos of mateship through cricket. In many ways the friendships we form off the field are just as important — if not more important — than the cricket we play on it.

Social Events & Tours

Beach cricket, black-tie balls and two to three overseas tours a season.

What’s on
We are very well known in Hong Kong cricket circles and beyond for our active social calendar, hosting events ranging from beach cricket tournaments to black-tie balls.

The Season Ahead

At the beginning of each season, your LCC Executive Committee (ExCom) is very busy — and this season has been no different. Our main focus has been Membership numbers, as we are heavily involved on three fronts.

We have retained our sponsors: Yardley Brothers for fine craft beer, HighLife our garment supplier, HK Electric, and of course Ashford Benjamin who continue as our title sponsor. We thank them all for their fantastic support.

In addition to the many cricket matches this season brings, we hope to provide a number of events to keep you occupied throughout the cricket season.
